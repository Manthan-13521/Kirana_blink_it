The controlled Mumbai probe measured search p95 **361 ms** at 32 concurrent requests over pooled HTTP/2 and **976 ms** with 32 new connections. In pooled search, D1 wall and SQL p95 were **218 ms** and **0.66 ms**. The no-D1 health control also showed a fresh TCP connection tail. Median emulated mobile time to first visible products improved from **1.97 to 1.41 seconds** across five samples per version, with a slower worst candidate sample. Platform queueing, other carriers and physical phones remain unmeasured. The **500 ms** mixed-workload p95 requirement stands. Accept an alternate traffic-specific threshold only after the terminal hosted workloads give a representative distribution and the owner explicitly approves it. [Performance evidence](testing/PERFORMANCE.md)

Cloudflare's 16:55 IST preflight reported **13,739 Worker requests, 279,568 D1 reads and 47,264 writes** account-wide, leaving 86,261 requests, 4,720,432 reads and 52,736 writes under the daily Free limits. This shared UTC day includes synthetic seeding and diagnostics. It cannot admit a protected 76,000-write test. Admission requires fewer than 14,000 current writes, 2.5 million reads and 40,000 requests. The test stops before 90,000 projected account writes, 3.5 million reads or 70,000 requests, including headroom for lagged metrics. At 16:52 IST, eight D1 databases used **9,072,640 bytes** in total. The merchant business DB used **479,232 bytes** and media DB **69,632 bytes**. Five Pages projects were visible and the merchant Worker had one ten-minute Cron schedule. [Limits and operational budgets](testing/CLOUDFLARE_USAGE.md)

Benchmarks have different scopes. The local 90-day history reached **169.6 MB** at 99,000 orders. A separate modeled sales day added **4.01 MB** to the business DB. From a small baseline these rates imply roughly 210 or 100 days respectively to the existing **400 MB** capacity warning. Use measured merchant growth for scheduling. Continue preserving financial history and start the rollover review when the warning appears or when the observed growth would reach it in 30 days.
