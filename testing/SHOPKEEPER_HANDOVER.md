# Running the store

1. Open the [owner dashboard](https://blink-it.pages.dev/owner) and choose **Sign in with Google**. Use your store owner account.
2. In **Catalog & stock**, choose **Add product**. Enter the product name, unique SKU, category, unit, selling price and opening stock. Choose a photo and save. Use **Edit** for prices or photos, and **Stock** to record stock received, spoiled or corrected.
3. In **Counter sales**, add products, choose the payment method, confirm the amount received and complete the sale. This deducts from the same stock used for online orders.
4. In **Orders**, open the incoming order, confirm it can be fulfilled, mark it as packing, then mark it ready. For pickup, mark the order complete when handed over and record any cash due. For delivery, assign the ready order to your active rider.
5. The rider opens the [rider dashboard](https://blink-it.pages.dev/rider). Use suggested or manual stop order, mark a delivery out and record its handover and any cash collected.
6. Use **Orders**, **Support & refunds** and **Reports** to check collections, invoices, pending payments, exceptions and sales. Verify a customer's charge in the Razorpay dashboard before resolving a reported discrepancy. Confirm the amount and payment status before any refund.
7. In **Store settings**, manage **Delivery & Pickup**, service windows, fees and ordering. Pause new orders during an interruption and continue existing orders and counter sales. Use **Team access** to invite or replace your rider.

At closing, reconcile cash with counter and order totals, check low stock and address any open payment or order discrepancies. Current checkout is restricted pending commercial acceptance.
